About me

I am a PhD student at the Research Center for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences, Shandong University, China, advised by Zhicong Lin. I am currently a visiting PhD student at Sungkyunkwan University, South Korea, working with Jang Soo Kim.

My research focuses on enumerative combinatorics, particularly bijections and statistics on permutations and trees. I am also interested in symmetric functions and orthogonal polynomials.
